---
'marking-menu': minor
---

Expose the menu to assistive technologies. The menu has the `menu` role and each item the `menuitem` role, named by its label. Items with a submenu set `aria-haspopup="menu"`. Wedges, connectors, strokes, and the opening indicator are hidden from the accessibility tree. The menu and its items are focusable from script but stay out of the Tab order.
