Wedges and plates now show a 2px inset outline by default, in
`hwb(0 45% 55%)`, so the wedge marks its activation area against a white
page. Outer connectors default to the same color instead of the plate
background. Style the outline with `--mm-wedge-outline-color`,
`--mm-wedge-outline-width`, `--mm-plate-outline-color`, and
`--mm-plate-outline-width`; set both widths to `0` to restore the previous
look.
